The process

From "I need a website" to a live link, in six moves.

Every order follows the same path. You make a few choices, hand us your business details, and we take it from there.

01

Choose your package

Pick the plan that matches how big a site your business needs.

02

Choose how you pay

Daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly. Monthly is the most common choice; yearly costs the least overall.

03

Choose your address

A free Fission address, or your own custom domain, which we buy and renew for you.

04

Pay online

Your order is created the moment payment is confirmed.

05

Tell us about your business

Name, logo, photos, services, prices, phone number, WhatsApp, address, and social accounts.

06

We build it and send you the link

We build your site, put it online, and message you when it's ready. From there, we keep it running and make changes as your business grows.

Steps 01–04 happen on this site. Steps 05 and 06 happen with a real person on our team.
